Bookmarking from the page ribbon

  • Outline ribbon = page not marked; filled = marked.
  • A tap on the ribbon always opens a menu: this page's bookmarks (click a name to rename, the bin removes), your other bookmarks to move here (top one first), and a field to add a new one. Hover any icon for what it does.
  • In the bookmarks list, click a bookmark to go to its page; the pencil renames it.
  • Reorder in the list by dragging the grip.

Final design. Tap the ribbon at the top of the page; click a bookmark's name to rename it; drag the grip to reorder (hover it for a hint), or focus it and press Alt + ↑/↓. Nothing is saved; "Start over" resets.
